Spectral features and MIRI's coverage

Each line is a spectral feature at its observed wavelength λrest(1 + z), colored by type; shaded bands are broad features such as the silicate absorption. Rest-frame mid-infrared features (PAHs, H₂, fine-structure lines, dust and ice) are in MIRI's range at low redshift, while the rest-frame optical and near-infrared lines (Hα, [O III], Paα and others) move into it for galaxies at z >rsim 3 to 10.
